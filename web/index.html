02 — How it works

Search, gather, deliver —
in three simple moves.

  1. 01

    Find the businesses

    Tell it a niche and a city. It works through every matching listing for you — page after page — until it reaches the number of leads you asked for.

  2. 02

    Capture the details

    It opens each business and pulls the details that matter — company name, phone, full address and website — checking each one as it goes so nothing slips through.

  3. 03

    Merge & deliver

    Everything lands in one clean spreadsheet. New finds are added, repeats are refreshed in place, and duplicates never make the cut — so your list only grows cleaner.
